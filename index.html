Questions

Things people often ask.

If you don't find what you're looking for here, please call or use the contact form — we're happy to answer any question, no matter how small.

Send a question →

How much does therapy cost?

−
Fees vary by therapy type and therapist. We aim to keep sessions affordable and accessible. Get in touch and we'll give you clear, straightforward pricing with no surprises.

How many sessions will I need?

+
There is no set number. Some clients find short-term help of 6–8 sessions sufficient; others come for much longer. We review progress together at six weeks and follow your lead throughout.

Do you offer online or telephone sessions?

+
Yes. We offer sessions by video call and telephone as well as face to face in Harrogate. Many clients prefer the convenience of online therapy and find it works just as well.

Is everything I say kept confidential?

+
Yes. Everything discussed is confidential. The only exceptions are situations where there is serious risk of harm to yourself or others. Your therapist will explain this fully in your first session.

What is the difference between counselling, CBT and coaching?

+
Counselling provides a space to explore feelings and experiences at your own pace. CBT is a structured approach focused on changing unhelpful thinking patterns. Life coaching is goal-oriented and focused on moving forward. We'll help you work out which approach best suits your situation.
